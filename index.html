A page about Liverpool football club

Liverpool is the English football team with the most Champions League trophies and the English team with the highest points total in one season. They are also tied first with the most English League Titles ever.

This photo is about Liverpool trophy parade in 2025 after winning the Premier League. Liverpool won the Premier League with 84 points, 10 ahead of the second place team that year. This was the first league title they got to celebrate infront of fans as their previous title was won behind closed doors due to the Covid-19 pandemic.

Three facts about Liverpool FC

  • Liverpool FC is the English team with the most Champions leagues, 6.
  • They were founded on June 3 1892 by John Houlding.
  • Despite Liverpool being known for wearing red, they originally wore blue and white for the first four years after being founded.
